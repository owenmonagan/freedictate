# Competitor Analysis

This is based on general market knowledge, not live SERP or DataForSEO data. Verify against SERPs before you publish any comparison claim.

| Competitor | Model | Strengths in SEO | Gap we exploit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wispr Flow | Subscription, cloud, cross-platform | Strong brand, funded PR, high domain authority, lots of "best dictation app" listicle placements | "free wispr flow alternative" and "wispr flow offline/privacy" searches |
| Superwhisper | Paid / pro tier, local + cloud models | A loved indie brand, many modes | "superwhisper alternative free" and "simpler than superwhisper" |
| VoiceInk | Paid, but source-available/open source | Owns "open source dictation mac" | Free with no license and zero config. Compare honestly |
| MacWhisper | Paid, mostly file transcription | Ranks for "whisper mac" | We do live push-to-talk, not files |
| Apple Dictation | Built in, free | Owns "how to dictate on mac" | Accuracy (Whisper large-v3), any button, works in every app the same way |

## Keyword gaps (opportunities)
- Competitor + "alternative" / "free" / "vs" pages. Every competitor gets searched this way, and we have no page for any of them.
- Mouse-button and push-to-talk dictation: almost nobody targets these head-on.
- "Mac dictation not working" and "dictation for coders / RSI": high-intent problem searches that competitors leave to forums.

## E-E-A-T comparison
The competitors have company About pages and press coverage. Our edge is transparency: open code, a visible maintainer, a changelog, and the fact that there's no cloud backend (which anyone can check in the code). Make that visible with an About/maintainer section, a link to the source, and a "how it works" architecture page.
