Changelog

Changelog

What changed in FreeDictate, newest first. The full history is on GitHub.

5 November 2026

Nothing heard, a Starting state, and a new look

  • If a recording is silent, the pill now says Nothing heard and nothing is pasted. Before, Whisper could turn silence into a stray "Thank you."
  • The pill shows a hollow ring until the microphone is actually open, so you know when to start talking.
  • A new aluminum-and-graphite look across the app and this site.

3 November 2026

First release

FreeDictate is out: one-button, free, offline dictation for Mac.

  • Hold any key, combo, modifier or mouse button to talk, or tap to start and tap again to stop. Esc cancels.
  • Speech is transcribed on your Mac by whisper.cpp with the large-v3 turbo model, through a local speech engine that starts at login.
  • Your words are pasted into the app you're using, and your clipboard is restored.
  • A small recording pill shows a live level meter while you talk.
  • One-command install and a clean uninstall.

Guided Setup

  • A step-by-step Setup window walks you through the microphone, Accessibility, the speech engine, start at login and choosing your button.
  • A new Try it step lets you dictate live and see your words before you finish.

Project notes

  • Added contributor documentation covering how FreeDictate works and its design.
