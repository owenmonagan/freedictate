New to FreeDictate?

FreeDictate is a free, open-source, MIT-licensed dictation app for Mac. You pick one button, hold it and talk, let go, and your words are pasted into whatever app has focus. There is no account, no subscription and no pro tier. Speech is turned into text on your Mac by whisper.cpp, so your voice never leaves the machine.
