FAQ

Is FreeDictate really free?

Yes. It's MIT-licensed open source. There's no subscription, no account, no trial, and no pro tier. You can read every line of the code on GitHub.

Does it need the internet?

Only to download it and the speech model once. After that, transcription runs offline with whisper.cpp on your Mac. Your audio is never uploaded.

How do I choose my button?

Pick any key, key combo, lone modifier like Right Option, or a middle, back, or forward mouse button. Tap it to start and tap again to stop, or hold it while you talk and let go.

What do I need to install it?

A Mac with Homebrew. The installer sets up Hammerspoon, ffmpeg, and whisper.cpp. Apple silicon is recommended for speed. Then allow Microphone and Accessibility access.

Which apps does it work in?

Any app with a text cursor: Mail, Slack, Notes, your code editor, a browser form. FreeDictate pastes the text where your cursor is, then restores your clipboard.

How is it different from Wispr Flow or Superwhisper?

Those are polished paid apps with modes, AI rewriting, and settings. FreeDictate does one thing: one button turns your voice into text, locally, for free. There's nothing to configure.

How do I cancel?

Press Esc while it's listening. Nothing is pasted.
