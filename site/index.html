Free voice to text that works everywhere.

FreeDictate is free voice to text for Mac. Hold a key, talk, and it types what you said in any app, more reliably than the dictation built into your Mac. Open source, and your voice never leaves your computer.

Download for Mac Free and open source, MIT
git clone https://github.com/owenmonagan/freedictate.git && cd freedictate && ./install.sh

Needs Homebrew. Paste it into Terminal.

Press and hold the key, then let go. Space works too.

Speech engine
whisper.cpp, running on your Mac's own chip. It listens only on 127.0.0.1, so nothing else on your network can reach it.
Model
Whisper large-v3 turbo, downloaded once during setup.
Your recordings
One temporary file in your private temp folder, replaced by the next dictation.
Network after setup
None. It works with Wi-Fi off. No account, no analytics.

Questions

Is FreeDictate really free?

Yes. It's MIT-licensed open source. There's no subscription, no account, no trial, and no pro tier. You can read every line of the code on GitHub.

Is free dictation on Mac any good?

It can be. FreeDictate's accuracy comes from Whisper large-v3 turbo running on your Mac, the open speech model many dictation apps build on. And it works the same way in every app, so you get the same results in Mail, Slack, your editor and your terminal. In our test of 40 synthetic-voice clips it had a 1.8% word error rate and returned text in under a second. See the test.

Does it need the internet?

Only to download it and the speech model once. After that, transcription runs offline with whisper.cpp on your Mac. Your audio is never uploaded.

How do I choose my button?

Pick any key, key combo, lone modifier like Right Option, or a middle, back, or forward mouse button. Tap it to start and tap again to stop, or hold it while you talk and let go.

What do I need to install it?

A Mac with Homebrew. The installer sets up Hammerspoon, ffmpeg, and whisper.cpp. Apple silicon is recommended for speed. Then allow Microphone and Accessibility access.

Which apps does it work in?

Any app with a text cursor: Mail, Slack, Notes, your code editor, a browser form. FreeDictate pastes the text where your cursor is, then restores your clipboard.

How is it different from Wispr Flow or Superwhisper?

Those are polished paid apps with modes, AI rewriting, and settings. FreeDictate does one thing: one button turns your voice into text, locally, for free. There's nothing to configure.

How do I cancel?

Press Esc while it's listening. Nothing is pasted.
